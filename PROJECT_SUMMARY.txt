📊 RESEARCH IMPLEMENTATION

   Dataset Characteristics:
   • 10,000 records across 5 sectors
   • 25 variables (demographic, work patterns, wellbeing)
   • 80% remote work representation
   • 67% longer work hours
   • Sector-specific stress and productivity patterns

   Hypothesis Tests Implemented:
   
   H1: Stress vs Productivity
       Test: Pearson Correlation + Linear Regression
       Result: SIGNIFICANT (p < 0.0001)
       
   H2: Sector Comparison
       Test: ANOVA + Kruskal-Wallis
       Result: SIGNIFICANT (p < 0.0001)
       
   H3: Technology Adaptation vs Productivity
       Test: Linear Regression
       Result: SIGNIFICANT (p < 0.0001)
       
   H4: Work Mode vs Stress
       Test: t-test + Chi-square
       Result: SIGNIFICANT (p = 0.012)
       
   H5: Work Hours vs Stress
       Test: Correlation
       Result: SIGNIFICANT (p < 0.0001)
       
   H6: Collaboration Challenges vs Productivity
       Test: ANOVA + Regression
       Result: NOT SIGNIFICANT (p = 0.052)
       
   H7: Job Security vs PRI
       Test: t-test + Regression
       Result: SIGNIFICANT (p < 0.0001)
       
   H8: Childcare vs Productivity
       Test: t-test + Chi-square
       Result: NOT SIGNIFICANT (p = 0.650)
       
   H9: Meetings vs Stress
       Test: Correlation
       Result: NOT SIGNIFICANT (p = 0.063)
       
   H10: Health Issues vs Productivity
       Test: t-test
       Result: NOT SIGNIFICANT (p = 0.096)

   Summary: 6 out of 10 hypotheses rejected (significant findings)

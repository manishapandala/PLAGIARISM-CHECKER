Fossil Echoes: Dinosaurs Through Deep Time

Museum exhibit prototype combining paleobiology, geologic eras, and the modern history of discovery.

Design (marks, channels, interactions): Each dinosaur is a custom "fossil glyph" (composed mark: body, tail, and spine ticks). Glyph position uses discovery year (x) and geologic period (y stratum). Glyph size maps to mass, fill color maps to diet, outline color maps to continent, and spine count maps to length. Hover reveals details, diet chips filter the exhibit, and a year slider reveals discoveries over time.

Custom visualization category: Combinatorial. This chart combines a stratigraphic timeline backdrop with a custom glyph system to form a new hybrid display, rather than using a single standard chart type.

Source modification note: Built from scratch for this assignment (no direct D3 block modified).
